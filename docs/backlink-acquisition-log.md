## Weekly process (each Monday)
1. Send the approved messages for the week (5 to 8), one at a time, from the business email.
2. Update `docs/backlink-prospects.csv` with status, date contacted and a follow-up date 7 to 10 days out.
3. Send one follow-up to anyone who hasn't replied after 7 to 10 days, then stop.
4. Check Bing Webmaster Backlinks and the Search Console Links report for new referring domains, and record them here with the exact URL.
5. Visit every acquired link: is it live, does it point to the right page, and is it nofollow, sponsored or dofollow? Mark it verified.
6. Add 5 or more new prospects from fresh research.
