What changes the cost of a session

  • How prepared you are. Lyrics finished, beat exported and parts rehearsed means fewer takes and fewer paid hours.
  • Session length. Booking a 2 or 3 hour block keeps you in the zone instead of rushing to finish in one hour.
  • Mixing and mastering. Recording, mixing and mastering are separate steps. Budget for all three if you're releasing the song.
  • How often you record. Paying per hour suits a one-off song. Recording every week usually makes a membership cheaper.
  • Extras. Video podcasting ($100) and live session performances ($75) are priced separately.
