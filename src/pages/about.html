About

Built for the ones who keep showing up.

Disco Sundays is a membership platform and recording studio in Hanover, Maryland.

Skills, community and real momentum.

Disco Sundays' membership is designed to help musicians, producers, DJs and creative entrepreneurs grow their skills and careers through structured courses, community support and practical projects.

That means more than studio time. Members' music is uploaded for A&R review, we help with booking shows, and our artist development sessions and Crash Test evaluation give every artist a clear plan for what comes next.

We're based in Hanover and work with independent partner studios, so members can keep creating without long contracts getting in the way.
